Exam format

AP Precalculus · May 2027 exam · 2 hours 55 minutes

Section I: Multiple choice
42 questions · 105 min · 62.5%
  • Part A: 29 questions, 65 min, no calculator
  • Part B: 13 questions, 40 min, graphing calculator required
Section II: Free response
4 questions · 70 min · 37.5%
  • Part A: 2 questions, 35 min, graphing calculator required (Q1 function concepts, Q2 modeling a non-periodic context)
  • Part B: 2 questions, 35 min, no calculator (Q3 modeling a periodic context, Q4 symbolic manipulations)
How you take it
Hybrid digital. You answer multiple choice in the Bluebook app. Free-response questions appear in Bluebook, and you handwrite your answers in a paper booklet.
What's tested
Units 1–3: polynomial and rational (30–40% of multiple choice), exponential and logarithmic (25–40%), trigonometric and polar (30–35%). Unit 4 (parameters, vectors, matrices) is taught in the course but not on the exam.
Reference sheet
None. You need to know the identities and rules yourself; this site's Reference button helps you learn them.
What changed
For May 2027, multiple choice Part A went from 28 questions in 80 minutes to 29 in 65, Part B from 12 to 13 questions, and each free-response part from 30 to 35 minutes.

Checked October 24, 2026 against College Board's published format. Formats can change, so confirm on AP Central before your exam.
