Which tool do I use?

Most of the time lost on the exam goes into choosing a model or a method. Learn the clues, then practice picking.

The intuition

Each problem leaves clues. Equal steps in x with equal differences in y is linear; equal ratios is exponential. Something that repeats is sinusoidal. An unknown in the exponent calls for a logarithm. A distance and an angle means polar. Name the model or method first; the algebra is the easy part.
