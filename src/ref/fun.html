Rates of change

[f(b) − f(a)] / (b − a)
average rate of change on [a, b]: slope of the secant
linear: constant rate of change
equal steps in x give equal differences in y
quadratic: rate of change changes at a constant rate
second differences are constant

Polynomials

p(x) = anxn + … + a1x + a0
degree n, leading term anxn
degree n ⇒ exactly n complex zeros (with multiplicity)
non-real zeros come in conjugate pairs a ± bi
(x − c)k: odd k crosses, even k touches
multiplicity at a real zero
at most n − 1 turning points
local extrema
even function f(−x) = f(x) · odd f(−x) = −f(x)
symmetry about the y-axis · about the origin

Rational functions r = p/q

deg p < deg q ⇒ y = 0
horizontal asymptote
deg p = deg q ⇒ y = ratio of leading coefficients
horizontal asymptote
deg p = deg q + 1 ⇒ slant asymptote
the quotient from long division
q(c) = 0, p(c) ≠ 0 ⇒ vertical asymptote x = c
after cancelling common factors
factor cancels completely ⇒ hole at x = c
height = simplified function at c

Equivalent forms

p(x) = q(x)·d(x) + r(x)
polynomial long division
(a + b)n = Σ C(n, k) an−kbk
binomial theorem (Pascal's triangle)

Transformations of y = f(x)

g(x) = a · f(b(x − h)) + k
vertical stretch a, horizontal stretch 1/|b|, shift right h, up k
a < 0 · b < 0
reflect over the x-axis · over the y-axis
